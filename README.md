# Q-PINO: Physics-Informed Quantum Neural Operators

Cayman Quinn and Jean-François Van Huele, Department of Physics and Astronomy, Brigham Young University

This project asks whether a physics-informed quantum neural operator (Q-PINO) can be designed so that its data-embedding circuit matches the spectral structure generated by a nonlinear PDE, here the Fisher–KPP equation. Variational quantum models are Fourier-type sums whose accessible frequencies come from the eigenvalue gaps of their data-encoding Hamiltonians. We use the PDE's spectral action to reverse-engineer encoding Hamiltonians whose frequencies line up with the dynamics the Q-PINO has to learn.
